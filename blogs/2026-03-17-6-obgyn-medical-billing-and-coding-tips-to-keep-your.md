While you focus on delivering great OBGYN care, billing is what turns that care into payment — it’s the bridge between clinical work and practice operations. Read on to explore key billing and coding considerations for obstetrics and gynecology, the steps of a successful billing process, and six tips to support your practice’s financial health. Understanding the importance of medical billing for OBGYN practices OBGYN billing is more complex than general medical billing due to strict payer rules and a wide range of services covered.
