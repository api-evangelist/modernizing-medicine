Don’t let staffing disruptions disrupt your cash flow Taking time off is essential for your team, but every staff absence — whether due to vacations, health reasons, or a resignation — can leave someone less experienced running your billing process. A lean staff doesn’t have to mean paused payments, though. Knowing which claims are at risk, cross-training your team, and regularly checking key metrics can help prevent a short-term staffing gap from becoming a long-term revenue problem.
