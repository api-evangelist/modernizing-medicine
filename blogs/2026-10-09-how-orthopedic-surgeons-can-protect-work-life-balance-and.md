Key takeaways Severe Time Pressure: Administrative burdens like remote EHR documentation and paperwork drain emotional reserves and slice into surgeons’ personal lives. Targeted Lifestyle Steps: Intentional scheduling, peer support networks, and structured self-care routines provide essential protection against professional exhaustion. Technology as an Ally: Transitioning to specialty-specific orthopedic software cuts evening charting time, helping surgeons leave work at the clinic.
