Key takeaways Telehealth is a broad umbrella term encompassing both clinical and nonclinical remote healthcare services. Telemedicine refers specifically to the remote delivery of clinical patient care, such as virtual consultations and post-op follow-ups. All telemedicine is telehealth, but not all telehealth is telemedicine.
